# Apollo 17 Lunar Module Challenger descent stage

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched December 7, 1972; reached the Moon December 11, 1972.
2. **Mission fact:** Apollo 17 was the last crewed mission to land on the Moon.
3. **What happened:** The crew left Challenger’s lower stage in Taurus-Littrow when its upper stage lifted off. The lower stage was built to stay on the Moon. **Status:** Left Behind.
