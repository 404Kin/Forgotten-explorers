# Apollo 16 Lunar Module Orion descent stage

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched April 16, 1972; reached the Moon April 20, 1972.
2. **Mission fact:** Apollo 16 landed in the Descartes highlands, not on a flat lunar plain.
3. **What happened:** The astronauts left Orion’s lower stage on the Moon after its upper stage lifted off. The lower stage was designed to remain there. **Status:** Left Behind.

## Think it through
