# Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched July 26, 1971; reached the Moon July 30, 1971.
2. **Mission fact:** Apollo 15’s rover helped astronauts travel about 28 kilometers across the Moon.
3. **What happened:** The astronauts deliberately parked the rover before leaving the Moon. This was the planned end of its job, not a failure. **Status:** Left Behind.

## Think it through

1. What was Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
