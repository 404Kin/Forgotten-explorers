# Chandrayaan-3 Vikram lunar lander

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched July 14, 2023; reached the Moon August 23, 2023.
2. **Mission fact:** Vikram landed at Shiv Shakti Point near the Moon’s south pole.
3. **What happened:** Vikram and its rover went to sleep as the lunar night began. ISRO tried to contact them after sunrise, but no connection was reported. **Status:** Went Silent.
