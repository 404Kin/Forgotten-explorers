# Apollo 16 Lunar Roving Vehicle

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched April 16, 1972; reached the Moon April 20, 1972.
2. **Mission fact:** The Apollo 16 rover carried astronauts across the Descartes highlands.
3. **What happened:** The astronauts deliberately parked the rover before leaving the Moon. This was the planned end of its job, not a failure. **Status:** Left Behind.

## Think it through

1. What was Apollo 16 Lunar Roving Vehicle's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Apollo 16 Lunar Roving Vehicle where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
