# Spirit Mars Exploration Rover

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched June 10, 2003; reached Mars January 4, 2004.
2. **Mission fact:** Spirit worked for more than six years, far beyond its planned 90 days.
3. **What happened:** Spirit became stuck in soft ground and could not tilt its solar panels toward the winter Sun. Its last signal reached Earth on March 22, 2010; NASA ended recovery efforts in 2011. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was Spirit Mars Exploration Rover's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Spirit Mars Exploration Rover where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
