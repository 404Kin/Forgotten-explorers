# Opportunity Mars Exploration Rover

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched July 7, 2003; reached Mars January 24, 2004.
2. **Mission fact:** Opportunity worked for almost 15 years, far longer than its planned 90 days.
3. **What happened:** A planet-wide dust storm blocked sunlight from Opportunity’s solar panels. Its last contact was June 10, 2018; NASA declared the mission over in 2019. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was Opportunity Mars Exploration Rover's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think
