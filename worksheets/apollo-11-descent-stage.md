# Apollo 11 Lunar Module Eagle descent stage

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched July 16, 1969; reached the Moon July 20, 1969.
2. **Mission fact:** Eagle’s lower stage became a launchpad for the astronauts’ ride home.
3. **What happened:** The astronauts left Eagle’s lower landing stage on the Moon when its upper stage lifted off. It was built to stay there. **Status:** Left Behind.

## Think it through

1. What was Apollo 11 Lunar Module Eagle descent stage's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Apollo 11 Lunar Module Eagle descent stage where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
