# Viking 1 Mars lander

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched August 20, 1975; reached Mars July 20, 1976.
2. **Mission fact:** Viking 1 sent the first clear pictures from the surface of Mars.
3. **What happened:** Viking 1 operated for more than six years. A faulty command sent from Earth interrupted communication on November 11, 1982, and contact could not be restored. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was Viking 1 Mars lander's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Viking 1 Mars lander where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
