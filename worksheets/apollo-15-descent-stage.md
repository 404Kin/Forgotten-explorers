# Apollo 15 Lunar Module Falcon descent stage

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched July 26, 1971; reached the Moon July 30, 1971.
2. **Mission fact:** Apollo 15 was the first Moon mission to use a lunar rover.
3. **What happened:** The crew left Falcon’s lower stage at Hadley Rille when its upper stage lifted off. The lower stage was intended to stay on the Moon. **Status:** Left Behind.
