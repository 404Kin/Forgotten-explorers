# Perseverance Mars 2020 rover

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched July 30, 2020; reached Mars February 18, 2021.
2. **Mission fact:** Perseverance collects rock samples that future missions may bring back to Earth.
3. **What happened:** Perseverance is still working in Jezero Crater. Its mission has not ended. **Status:** Still Talking.

## Think it through

1. What was Perseverance Mars 2020 rover's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Perseverance Mars 2020 rover where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
