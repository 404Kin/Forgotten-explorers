# Viking 2 Mars lander

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched September 9, 1975; reached Mars September 3, 1976.
2. **Mission fact:** Viking 2 landed on a broad plain called Utopia Planitia.
3. **What happened:** Viking 2’s batteries lost power after nearly four years on Mars. Its final data reached Earth on April 11, 1980. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was Viking 2 Mars lander's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Viking 2 Mars lander where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
