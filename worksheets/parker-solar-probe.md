# Parker Solar Probe

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched August 12, 2018; reached deep space April 28, 2021.
2. **Mission fact:** Parker flies through the Sun’s outer atmosphere, protected by a heat shield.
3. **What happened:** Parker Solar Probe is still making close passes by the Sun and sending science data to Earth. Its mission has not ended. **Status:** Still Talking.

## Think it through

1. What was Parker Solar Probe's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Parker Solar Probe where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
