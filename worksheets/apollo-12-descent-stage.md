# Apollo 12 Lunar Module Intrepid descent stage

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched November 14, 1969; reached the Moon November 19, 1969.
2. **Mission fact:** Intrepid landed about 535 feet from Surveyor 3, a robot that had arrived two years earlier.
3. **What happened:** The astronauts left Intrepid’s lower stage behind when the upper stage lifted off. The lower stage was made to stay on the Moon. **Status:** Left Behind.

## Think it through

1. What was Apollo 12 Lunar Module Intrepid descent stage's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Apollo 12 Lunar Module Intrepid descent stage where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
