# InSight Mars lander

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched May 5, 2018; reached Mars November 26, 2018.
2. **Mission fact:** InSight listened for marsquakes to learn about the inside of Mars.
3. **What happened:** Dust covered InSight’s solar panels until the lander could not make enough power. Its last message reached Earth on December 15, 2022. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was InSight Mars lander's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw InSight Mars lander where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
