# Ingenuity Mars Helicopter

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched July 30, 2020; reached Mars February 18, 2021.
2. **Mission fact:** Ingenuity made 72 flights on Mars, even though it was planned for just five.
3. **What happened:** During its 72nd flight, Ingenuity lost contact while descending and hit the ground hard. The landing damaged its rotor blades, so it could not fly again; it continued sending some data through Perseverance afterward. **Status:** Still Talking.

## Think it through

1. What was Ingenuity Mars Helicopter's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Ingenuity Mars Helicopter where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
