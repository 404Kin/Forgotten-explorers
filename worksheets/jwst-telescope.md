# James Webb Space Telescope

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched December 25, 2021; reached deep space January 24, 2022.
2. **Mission fact:** Webb’s main mirror has 18 gold-coated segments.
3. **What happened:** Webb is still operating near the Sun-Earth L2 point. Its mission has not ended. **Status:** Still Talking.

## Think it through

1. What was James Webb Space Telescope's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw James Webb Space Telescope where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
