# Surveyor 3 lunar lander

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched April 17, 1967; reached the Moon April 20, 1967.
2. **Mission fact:** Apollo 12 astronauts later walked up to Surveyor 3 and brought some parts back to Earth.
3. **What happened:** Surveyor 3 used solar power. It stopped sending signals after lunar night began, when its panels could not get sunlight. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was Surveyor 3 lunar lander's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Surveyor 3 lunar lander where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
