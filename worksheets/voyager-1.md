# Voyager 1

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched September 5, 1977; reached deep space August 25, 2012.
2. **Mission fact:** Voyager 1 carries a golden record with sounds and pictures from Earth.
3. **What happened:** Voyager 1 crossed into interstellar space and still sends data to Earth. Its mission has not ended. **Status:** Still Talking.

## Think it through

1. What was Voyager 1's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Voyager 1 where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
