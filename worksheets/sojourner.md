# Sojourner Mars rover

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched December 4, 1996; reached Mars July 4, 1997.
2. **Mission fact:** Sojourner worked for 83 days, much longer than its planned seven days.
3. **What happened:** Sojourner depended on its Pathfinder lander to relay messages. Pathfinder’s last data reached Earth on September 27, 1997, and the rover fell silent with it. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was Sojourner Mars rover's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Sojourner Mars rover where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
