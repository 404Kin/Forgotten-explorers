# Phoenix Mars lander

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched August 4, 2007; reached Mars May 25, 2008.
2. **Mission fact:** Phoenix used a robotic arm to uncover water ice beneath the Martian soil.
3. **What happened:** Phoenix’s solar panels stopped working in the dark Martian winter. The lander could no longer send messages. **Status:** Went Silent.

## Think it through

1. What was Phoenix Mars lander's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Phoenix Mars lander where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
