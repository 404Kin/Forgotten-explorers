# Apollo 14 Lunar Module Antares descent stage

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched January 31, 1971; reached the Moon February 5, 1971.
2. **Mission fact:** Antares landed in the Fra Mauro highlands, a hilly area of the Moon.
3. **What happened:** The astronauts left Antares’s lower stage behind when the upper stage lifted off. The landing stage was built to remain on the Moon. **Status:** Left Behind.
