# Curiosity Mars Science Laboratory rover

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched November 26, 2011; reached Mars August 6, 2012.
2. **Mission fact:** Curiosity is about the size of a car and explores Mount Sharp.
3. **What happened:** Curiosity is still operating and exploring Mars. Its mission has not ended. **Status:** Still Talking.

## Think it through

1. What was Curiosity Mars Science Laboratory rover's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Curiosity Mars Science Laboratory rover where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
