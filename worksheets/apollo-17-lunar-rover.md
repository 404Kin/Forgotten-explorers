# Apollo 17 Lunar Roving Vehicle

**Explorer file — printable activity page**

## Three fact boxes

1. **Journey:** Launched December 7, 1972; reached the Moon December 11, 1972.
2. **Mission fact:** Apollo 17’s rover covered about 35 kilometers, the longest lunar rover trip of Apollo.
3. **What happened:** The astronauts deliberately parked the rover before leaving the Moon. This was planned, not a rover failure. **Status:** Left Behind.

## Think it through

1. What was Apollo 17 Lunar Roving Vehicle's job?
2. Why is it still at its destination or travelling in space?
3. Which fact would you tell a friend?

## Draw and think

Draw Apollo 17 Lunar Roving Vehicle where it explored. Add one detail from the mission fact box. What question would you ask a scientist about this explorer?
