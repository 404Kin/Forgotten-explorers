## 3D models (checked 2026-11-13)

Files come from [NASA 3D Resources](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources) (free to use, credit NASA) and, for Curiosity, NASA/JPL [Eyes on the Solar System](https://eyes.nasa.gov/apps/solar-system/). They were shrunk with gltf-transform (meshopt + WebP textures, each under 3 MB) and load lazily from `public/models/`. A robot NASA has no model for shows a plain message instead; **no model is drawn or generated by us**.
